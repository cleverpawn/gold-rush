How to Play

"Listen up, partner — Pappy hid one gold nugget in every colored claim. Find 'em all!" — Sadie

  1. 🗺️ One nugget per claim. Every colored territory hides exactly one gold nugget.
  2. ⛏️ One per row & column. Each row and each column holds exactly one nugget too.
  3. 🚫 Nuggets never touch — not side-by-side, not even corner-to-corner!
  4. ❤️ Three hearts. Digging an empty shaft costs one. Lose all three and the mine plays out — but the nuggets you've found stay found.
  5. 👆 Tap a cell to mark a survey X. Double-tap to dig for gold. Drag across cells to survey a whole line.
  6. 💡 Hints ask Sadie to point at a real nugget (3 per claim).
